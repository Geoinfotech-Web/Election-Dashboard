Frequently asked questions

Start with the essentials.

Learn what the dashboard covers, where its information comes from, and how to use it responsibly.
What is the Geospatial Intelligence Election Dashboard?

It is an interactive platform for exploring election results, population and PVC indicators, and polling-unit accessibility through maps and data visualisations.

Can I explore information below country level?

Yes. Where data is available, the platform can support country, region, district, ward, and polling-location views.

Are dashboard figures official final results?

Always check the source and status shown with each dataset. Where applicable, official election declarations remain the authoritative record.

Can I download or share what I find?

Reporting and share tools are being expanded progressively. Existing pages provide the available export options for their datasets.
